The hard parts, done

Everything between "hello world" and your first paying user.

Getting auth, a paywall and payments to work across a popup, a service worker and content scripts is the tedious part. It's already built and tested.

⚡

Manifest V3 with hot reload

React, TypeScript and Tailwind on Vite. The manifest is compiled for you and the popup and content scripts reload as you code.

🔐

Auth that survives the service worker

Supabase email auth with the session stored in chrome.storage. Sign-up confirmation links land on a page inside your extension and sign the user in.

★

Paywall in one line

Ask CHECK_PRO_STATUS from anywhere. Free users get a polished upgrade modal; Pro users get the feature.

💳

Payments via webhooks

Stripe or Lemon Squeezy, your pick. Supabase Edge Functions verify each webhook signature and upgrade or downgrade the user, including refunds and cancellations.

🔁

Typed messaging

Popup, background and content scripts talk through one typed message map. Wrong payloads fail at compile time, not in production.

🧩

UI that never breaks the page

In-page UI renders in a Shadow DOM with its own Tailwind styles, so host sites can't break your UI and your CSS can't break theirs.

FAQ

Questions

Who is ExtensionKit for?

Developers who want to sell a Chrome extension, whether that's a side project, a micro-SaaS or a client project, without spending weeks wiring auth and payments into Manifest V3.

Do I need a Stripe account?

No. Stripe and Lemon Squeezy are both supported, and you switch between them with one line in .env. Lemon Squeezy is a good option if Stripe isn't available in your country.

What exactly do I get?

The complete source code plus setup guides for Supabase, Stripe and Lemon Squeezy. You own your copy and can change anything.

Can I use it in multiple projects?

Yes, including client work. The only thing you can't do is resell or redistribute the boilerplate itself.
