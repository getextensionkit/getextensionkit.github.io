ReadStats support

Reading time and readability for any page.

Need help? Email extensionkit.help@gmail.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a day.

How to use it

  • Open any article. The button in the bottom-right corner shows its reading time.
  • Click the button for the word count and, with Pro, the readability report.
  • Drag the button up or down to move it. Hover it and click ✕ to hide it on a site; bring it back from the extension's popup.

Pro

  • Pro adds a readability score, a US school grade level and your three longest sentences.
  • Upgrade from the extension: click the ReadStats icon in Chrome's toolbar, sign in, then Upgrade to Pro.
  • Manage or cancel your plan from the popup with Manage billing.

Common questions

  • The button doesn't appear. Reload the page. Chrome doesn't let extensions run on chrome:// pages or the Chrome Web Store.
  • I paid but I'm not Pro yet. Close and reopen the popup. If it's still Free after a few minutes, email me with your order number.
  • How do I delete my account? Popup → Delete account. Your account and data are erased.
